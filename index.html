STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you ask.

Is there alcohol?+

No. It is an alcohol-free event, with soft drinks and refreshments available. That is a deliberate part of the format rather than a restriction: the room stays present and the music does the work.

Is the breathwork compulsory?+

No. The guided breathing opens the session and you are welcome to take part, sit it out, lie down or go at your own pace. Nobody is watching, and you can join the floor whenever you like.

What does rave entry include?+

Rave entry is built around the music: a short guided introduction to the breathing, then the floor for the rest of the session. The full guided SOMA Breath experience is a separate ticket, and we will set both out clearly when tickets are released.

Is this a daytime event?+

Yes. It runs during the day and you are done and home in good time. Full timings go out with the venue announcement.

Who is it for?+

Anyone who loves electronic music and fancies experiencing it differently. All ages, first timers welcome, and no dance or breathwork experience needed. Many people come on their own and some with friends. Either way, you can expect a very warm welcome.

When are tickets released?+

January 2027, in London. The waitlist hears the line-up, the venue and the ticket release before anyone else, and gets first access when tickets go live.

Is the venue accessible?+

Tell us what you need at info@houseofjung.org and we will confirm exactly what the venue can offer before you book. Full access details go out with the venue announcement.
