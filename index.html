HOW THE ROOM RUNS

Three gears.
No warm-up act.

  1. 01

    Breathe

    Twenty minutes on the floor with a guide and a sound system. This is the part that does what a drink used to.

  2. 02

    Lift

    The breath holds hit and the room changes. Nothing taken. All of it your own body.

  3. 03

    Go

    Headliners, full system, a room already wide open. Two hours, and you are home in good time.

The high.
Not the hangover.

Everyone who has done this says the same thing: they did not miss the drink.

Not because they were being good. Because by the time the music comes up, the breathing has already taken them somewhere a drink never did, and the thought of blunting it feels mad.

You leave clear. You remember all of it. There is no tomorrow to write off.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you ask.

Is there really no bar?+

None. Soft drinks and refreshments, and that is it. Nobody is checking what you did last night, and nobody is drinking at this one.

Do I have to do the breathwork?+

No, but it is the bit that makes the rest work. You can sit it out, lie down, or go at a tenth of the pace. Nobody is watching.

Is this a night out?+

No. It runs in the daytime and you are done and home in good time. The session itself is around two hours.

Who is it for?+

All ages, first timers welcome. Many people come on their own, and some with friends. Either way, you can expect a very warm welcome.

When do tickets go live?+

We announce the line-up and the venue to the list before anyone else, then the ticket drop. 280 places across two sessions, so the list gets the first shot.
